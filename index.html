Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my shipment?

You can track your shipment using your tracking number in the search box above, or access real-time updates through your account dashboard.

What are the delivery times?

Delivery times vary by service: Air freight 1-3 days, Sea freight 7-21 days, Trucking 2-5 days. Rush options available.

Is my cargo insured?

Premium and Professional plans include insurance. Basic plan requires optional add-on insurance for full coverage.

What items cannot be shipped?

Hazardous materials, explosives, and perishable goods require special handling. Contact us for specific item restrictions.
